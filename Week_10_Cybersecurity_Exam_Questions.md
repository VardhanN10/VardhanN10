## Study Tips

1. **Focus on understanding concepts, not memorization** - The exam tests your ability to recognize concepts from detailed descriptions
2. **Note how descriptions expand on slide content** - Slides provide brief bullet points; exam questions elaborate with context
3. **Pay attention to distinguishing characteristics** - Each description includes specific details that differentiate it from similar concepts
4. **Practice with acronyms** - Many answers are abbreviations; know both the acronym and full term
5. **Review related concepts together** - Questions are grouped by topic to help you see relationships
